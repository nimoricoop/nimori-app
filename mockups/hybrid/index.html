Manual

How to play NIMORI

Providing liquidity has always been a solo game: you need both sides of a pair. NIMORI makes it a two-player mode. One asset per player, one Uniswap v4 position, two seats.

LobbyDifficultySessionUnplugExampleRisksParameters

1 · The lobby

Each pair has two queues. 1P deposits ETH, 2P deposits the token. A deposit waits until the other side can match it, FIFO by value, at the pool price (TWAP checked against spot). Waiting earns nothing and can be withdrawn anytime, free. If sizes differ, the larger deposit is partly filled and the rest stays queued.

2 · Difficulty

You are only matched with a player on the same difficulty.

3 · The session

A match opens one v4 position and mints two Seat NFTs, 1P and 2P. A seat is a save file: it stores side, difficulty, entry prices and the fee split, and it can be sold. Fees split 50 / 50 by default; the scarce side can get a seat bonus up to 70 / 30. 10% of trading fees go to Arcade stakers first.

4 · Unplug, hot swap, rage quit

After 24 h either player can unplug. The mover rule applies: the asset whose USD price moved more is the mover. The non-mover gets back exactly what they deposited, in their own asset, as long as the position covers it. The mover gets the rest. Fees are added on top.

If someone is waiting on the same side and difficulty, they hot swap into the empty seat and the position stays open. Leaving before 24 h is a rage quit: 1% of seat value goes to your partner.

5 · Worked example

ETH $2,000, token $0.10, Easy. 1P deposits 1 ETH, 2P deposits 20,000 tokens. Position $4,000.

Fees are added in both cases. In a much deeper token crash (around −75%) the position no longer covers the 1P deposit and the cap applies: 1P receives the whole position, which is less than 1 ETH.

6 · Risks

  • Smart contract risk. Code can have bugs. Contracts are not deployed; audits will be published before launch.
  • Mover risk. If your asset moves more than your partner's, you carry the IL.
  • Coverage cap. The non-mover is repaid only as long as the position covers it.
  • Range risk (Hard). Narrow positions can go out of range and stop earning.
  • Oracle risk. Bad price data could misassign IL. Mitigated by TWAP checks and staleness guards.
  • Lobby wait. If the other queue is empty, your deposit earns nothing.
